| 2026-09-09 | Mohit | Claude Code (Opus) | Fix two detector defects: rep windows that never close, and a smoothing filter whose length depended on the device sample rate | Two issues were found when replaying the wider trace set. First, when a phone is left still after a set, its reading stays around 9.81, which is between lowThreshold (9.7) and highThreshold (10.15). This meant a rep window could stay open indefinitely, with 36.9 seconds of a stationary phone being counted as one squat. The fix was to check for this condition while the detector is running rather than only when the window closes. Second, the moving average used 25 samples, which meant the actual smoothing time changed depending on the device sample rate. It was 250 ms at 100 Hz, but 430 ms on the Samsung trace and 500 ms on the live 50 Hz stream. The filter was changed to use timestamps so that its behaviour follows the observed sample rate. This required changing minAmplitude from 0.75 to 0.94, and duration was no longer used as a separate guard because the 250 ms wobble could last 743 ms, which is longer than the shortest genuine rep at 615 ms. The trade-off is documented in the KDoc. Two outdated comments in the synthetic-trace test were also corrected. |
